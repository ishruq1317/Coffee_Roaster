2. Creative & UI/UX Standards: Avoiding "AI Slop" 
Clients can spot low-effort, AI-generated website templates instantly. All demos must look like 
custom-coded, boutique agency products with bespoke art direction. 
Anti-"AI Slop" Design Rules 
1.​ Forbidden Color Palettes: 
○​ Strictly forbid generic saturated indigo-to-purple gradients, generic cyan glow on 
dark #0f172a, and cookie-cutter SaaS aesthetics. 
2.​ Mandatory Custom Palettes: 
○​ Every industry must have a dedicated, real-world palette: 
■​ Dental: Deep clinical forest green (#0F2E28), clean warm ecru (#F9F6F0), 
sterile ice blue accents (#DDEAEB). 
■​ Bistro/Bakery: Warm espresso, stone ivory, terracotta, butter yellow, and 
deep Bordeaux wine accents. 
■​ Salon/Spa: Warm sand, alabaster, soft olive, charcoal slate, and brushed 
brass. 
■​ Adventure/Travel: Granite gray, safety orange accent, alpine moss, and 
high-contrast chalk white. 
3.​ Typography Rigor: 
○​ Pair an authentic editorial display font (e.g., Playfair Display, Fraunces, Syne, Clash 
Display, or Cinzel) with an ultra-clean sans-serif for UI/body copy (e.g., Plus Jakarta 
Sans, General Sans, or Inter). Never use default system fonts or mismatched type 
scales. 
Advanced Visual Aesthetics & Surface Depth 
●​ Glassmorphism & Frosted Glass: Use subtle backdrop-filter: blur(16px) with a 1px 
border (border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)) and multi-layered inner shadows. 
Avoid heavy, muddy blurs. 
●​ Liquid & Water Morphism: Clean refracted surface highlights, liquid pill buttons with soft 
outer ambient glow, and translucent floating badge indicators. 
●​ Neomorphism / Soft Neu-Depth: Reserve exclusively for tactile controls (e.g., toggle 
switches, date pickers, segmented controls). Keep contrast ratios high enough to comply 
with accessibility standards. 
●​ Asymmetrical & Editorial Layouts: Break out of standard 3-box card rows. Use 
overlapping hero cards, sticky sidebars, high-fashion magazine grids, and staggered 
image collages. 
3. Motion, Micro-Interactions & Interactivity 
Static websites do not impress clients on video calls or mobile screens. Every demo must feel 
active, responsive, and dynamic. 
Required Motion Elements 

--- Page 3 ---
●​ Scroll-Triggered Reveals: Smooth stagger reveals for text blocks and cards (using 
Framer Motion, GSAP, or CSS @keyframes). Avoid abrupt, jittery entrance effects. 
●​ Magnetic & Dynamic Buttons: Action buttons that subtly attract the cursor or feature 
liquid wave fills upon hover. 
●​ Card Depth & Tilt: 3D perspective tilts (rotateX, rotateY) on key showcase cards reacting 
to mouse movement. 
●​ Seamless State Transitions: Accordions, mobile menus, and modals must transition 
smoothly with cubic-bezier easing (ease-out, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)). 
Mandatory Interactive Features Per Site 
●​ Live Calculation / Filter Tool: 
○​ Every demo must contain at least one interactive calculation tool (e.g., cost 
estimator, service duration calculator, or group tour discount selector). 
●​ Interactive Booking/Reservation Drawer: 
○​ Clicking "Book Now" or "Reserve" must open a slide-out drawer or polished modal 
with functional steps (date selection, personal detail fields, confirmation state). 
●​ Before / After or Showcase Comparer: 
○​ For clinics, salons, and hospitality: A smooth drag-to-slide visual comparison or 
interactive gallery view. 
4. Technical Performance & Network Optimization 
A flashy demo is useless if it lags on a prospect’s mobile phone. European business owners will 
test links on 4G networks while on the go. 
Network & Metric Targets (Google PageSpeed / Lighthouse) 
●​ Performance Score: 90+ on both Mobile and Desktop. 
●​ Largest Contentful Paint (LCP): Under 1.8 seconds. 
●​ First Input Delay (FID) / Interaction to Next Paint (INP): Under 100 milliseconds. 
●​ Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS): 0.00 (Strictly zero content jumping as images or fonts 
load). 
Optimization Checklist 
1.​ Images: Convert all photography to .webp or .avif. No image file should exceed 200 KB. 
2.​ Responsive Images: Use <picture> or srcset so small phones never load desktop 4K 
hero images. 
3.​ Font Loading: Use font-display: swap; and preload key weights to prevent layout shifts. 
4.​ Code Delivery: Eliminate bloated third-party dependencies. Clean vanilla JavaScript or 
lightweight bundled components only. 
5.​ Skeleton Loaders: Add subtle shimmer skeleton placeholders while high-resolution 
media loads.
